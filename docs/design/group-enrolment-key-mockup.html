Group enrolment keys

Mockups for enrol_apply: an applicant holding a valid group key fills in the application and is enrolled at once, in that group, without waiting for a decision. A wrong key is never a dead end: the applicant is offered the ordinary route. Where local_groupdist is installed, each group's own seats count too. Plan and decisions: group-enrolment-key.md (G1–G15). Each screen shows the recommended option.

0The five outcomes

Eligibility is unchanged: the window, the cohort, "new enrolments" and the applicant limit refuse a key holder like anybody else. The key only replaces the decision, and it is checked again inside the submission lock, from the database. Two capacities can stop an admission: the course's places, and, with local_groupdist, the seats of every group the admission would join.

Valid key, a place and a seat freeEnrolled now, active, joins the key's group plus the instance's groups. Managers get an informational message.
Valid key, course places fullBecomes a normal application, flagged "valid key, no place". The approver's group chooser pre-ticks the key's group. (G4)
Valid key, group out of seatsOnly with local_groupdist. Becomes a normal application, flagged "valid key, group full: Turma B". (G14)
Wrong keyForm error. Fix it, or tick "send without the key" to apply for approval. (G6)
No keyExactly today's route: pending until a manager decides.

8Group seats from local_groupdist

Seats are groupdist's group custom field (empty = no limit, 0 = no seats), and a seat is taken by every member of the group, as on groupdist's bulk edit page (G13). On the key route they are a gate; for a person approving they are information (G15). Without groupdist none of this appears and nothing changes.
