One application, read in order

The single-application review page holds every fact a decider needs and says none of them in the order the decision asks for them. This page diagnoses that order, proposes five concepts with desktop and phone mockups, and recommends one to build now and one to keep for later. Nothing here is built.

The owner, 5 November 2026

The owner names four questions the page does not answer in order: where to start reading, what weighs on the decision, what each action results in, and what can be adjusted. Those four, plus the act itself, are the spine of every concept below.

Chosen (owner, 2026-11-05)

A. Decision funnel

One column of falling weight: who and how, then what weighs (traffic-light signals with numbers), then what confirming does, in words, then the decision bar. Adjustments fold away. Same form, same POST contract. One slice, tier 2 with Behat.

Later, maybe

B. Master-detail

The queue on the left, A's funnel on the right. Fast for a batch, expensive in Moodle, and it reopens two settled decisions (where a decision lands, and the walk). Phase 2, built around A's pane, only if usage asks.

Take the colours, not the layout

C. The reference mockup, fixed

Its chips, bars and tinted history card read fast and carry straight into A. Its equal-weight cards do not tell the eye where to start, which is the owner's first complaint.

Not now

D. Inline triage in the queue

Good for clean, keyed batches, but it collides with the queue's bulk form and with the review page's cancel confirmation. If ever built: confirm and defer only, cancel stays on the full page.

Fold into A

E. An attention line, never a verdict

One line that counts the signals and names the worst one. Never "ready to confirm": the plugin cannot see what the decider is judging, and a verdict invites rubber-stamping.

§02 · why the page reads slowly

Diagnosis: the decider's five questions, and where today's page answers them

A decider opening one application asks, in this order:

  1. Who is this, in what state, and how did they arrive? Status, the key and its group, how long it has waited, through which method.
  2. Can I approve? The signals that weigh: seats left in the target group, places left on the method, earlier applications to this course, whether the details the method asked for are there, and any warning. Each is pass, attention or over the limit.
  3. What happens if I choose each action? Confirm: who joins which group with which role, and that the applicant is told. Defer: the waiting list, which keeps holding a place against the applications limit. Cancel: the application ends and the enrolment is removed, after a confirmation.
  4. What can I adjust? Groups, role, a message to the applicant, a note for the record. Most decisions adjust nothing, so this is disclosed, not shown.
  5. Act.

§03 · read from the repo's CLAUDE.md and the code

Contracts no concept may break

  • The form posts the queue's own contract: formaction, a non-empty userenrolments[], sesskey; groups[], roleid, outcomemessage, decisionnote beside them. manage.php has no branch of its own for this page, and must not grow one.
  • Cancel is first in source order, and that is why cancel asks. The first submit is the form's default for Enter; manage.php:224 intercepts cancel from this page without confirmed and asks. Reordering the buttons without moving the interception would let Enter in a chooser enrol the applicant. Visual order may differ from source order only through the row's own flex layout, as today.
  • The sticky footer is a fixed box (about 80 px) whose content clips with no scrollbar, and the row does not wrap. Only the buttons go in it; a second line per button must fit, and on a phone it must not.
  • Message and note never blur. outcomemessage reaches the applicant; decisionnote never does. The stored note is shown, never pre-filled into the box (an empty box clears it on the next decision). Coming back from the cancel confirmation, both boxes carry what was typed.
  • One identity rule through \enrol_apply\local\identity (the e-mail address only when the site's showuseridentity names it); the snapshot is read frozen and never from the live profile; withheld fields are dropped whether or not they hold a value, because a marker that appears only where there is data is a presence oracle.
  • Gates per panel: earlier applications on viewreports, with no empty heading for a reader without it; capacity on manageapplications in the course; the key group's name only to a reader allowed to see that group; choosers on choosedecision, the role list on what the reader may assign; a mentor sees no group name.
  • The walk comes from queue::scope(), never from the request, and visits exactly what the queue lists, in its order. The wrapper shows on hasnav, not on the neighbours.
  • A decision lands on the queue, all three alike, because two of them leave this url with nothing to decide.
  • Seats and places warn, never block (R5, places_full() advisory). A red signal never disables Confirm.
  • An empty group choice is not "no group". record_decided_groups() writes the empty choice and chosen_groups() reads it back as "no choice", so add_instance_groups() falls back to default_approval_groups(): for a keyed application, the key's group plus the method's. Any chooser that lets every box be unticked must say so where the boxes are.
Found on the way, and true of today's page too

Deselecting every option in today's multi-select reads as "join no group" and does the opposite for a keyed application: the approval joins the key's group anyway (lib.php:1016–1019, classes/local/submission.php:775–778). Today's help text says "Deixe em branco para usar os grupos configurados no método de inscrição", which omits the key's group. Concept A's outcome sentence names the fallback groups whenever nothing is ticked.

§05b · the chosen concept, at course scale

A with dozens of groups

“Realmente gostei mais da opção A, o que faltou agora é quando há dezenas de grupos. Por exemplo, tenho caso com quase 100 grupos no curso. Como isso pode ser tratado no design?”

The owner, 5 November 2026

Concept A as drawn above shows every group the reader may choose as a card. Today's chooser does the same in a select multiple: decision_controls_context() offers every group groupscope::choosable_groups() returns (renderer.php:647). With 96 groups that is 96 cards, or a native list a decider scrolls with control-click. The answer below keeps the funnel exactly as it is and changes one thing: the groups the approval will join stay as cards, and every other group is reached by searching.

Where the many groups actually are

Most decisions never open "Ajustar". The outcome card already names the destination (the key's group, the method's groups), and Confirm does exactly that. So a course of 100 groups changes nothing in the strip, the signals, the outcome card or the bar. The count matters in three places only:

  • "Ajustar grupos e papel" on the review page, when the decider adds or moves a group.
  • The participants page's bulk decision form (classes/form/bulk_decision_form.php), whose group field is a moodleform select of every choosable group today (bulk_decision_form.php:165–176).
  • The queue's group filter, proposed in V2 of group-screens.html and not yet built.

All three get the same control and the same option list, so a decider learns it once.

RP-D3ThresholdsOk below 80 %; attention at 80 % or more (four fifths, the queue meter's rule, renderer.php:543); over the limit at 100 % (members ≥ seats, the chooser's "lotado" rule, renderer.php:660). Seats or places without a limit: no row, and "sem limite de vagas" in the method line. Earlier applications are "Leia", never red.
RP-D4Does a red signal ever disable a decision?No (R5, places_full() advisory). Confirm stays enabled; its consequence line names the overbooking ("Turma A ficará com 21 de 20"), and manage.php's warnings after the decision stay.
RP-D5Is the outcome card the default view, with "Ajustar" collapsed?Yes. Open by default only when a preselected group is over the limit, or the method gives no group and the reader may choose. A small AMD module keeps the sentence in step with ticks.
RP-D6Group chooser: checkbox cards instead of a multi-select?Yes, same groups[] name and allowlist. Under the cards, the empty-choice sentence names the groups an empty choice really joins (the key's group and the method's), because unticking all is not "no group".
RP-D7Message and noteOne disclosure "Escrever ao solicitante ou anotar para o registro", collapsed, and open in the markup whenever either box holds a value (the way back from the cancel confirmation). The stored note stays shown in the strip, never pre-filled.
RP-D8Button consequence wording, and where it livesBeside each button, not inside it. Confirm: "Turma A, como Estudante, e recebe o aviso" (en "Joins Turma A as Student and is notified"). Defer: "Vai para a lista de espera e recebe o aviso"; for an already-deferred one, "Continua na lista; avisa só se houver mensagem". Cancel: "Encerra e remove a inscrição; pede confirmação". On phones, short labels in the bar and the three lines in the body above it.
RP-D9Evidence column: right, or below?Right at desktop widths (a 4-of-12 column after the funnel in source order), below on narrower screens and collapsed on phones. Every signal that has evidence links to it.
RP-D10The key chip's textA short form in the strip, "Chave aceita: Turma A", because the status chip beside it already says it awaits review; the long keyoutcomeforreview stays on the queue and the report.
RP-D11Show how long it has waited?Yes: "Solicitada há 2 dias (03/11/26, 09:14)", from the application date, in the strip.
RP-D12The viewer's funnelStrip with a "Somente leitura" chip and the existing sentence; signals without seats, places or the method line (capacity stays a decider's, V-D3); no outcome card and no bar.
RP-D13After a decision: the queue, or the next application?The queue, as today, for A. Revisit only with B, where "Confirmar e ir para a próxima" is the point.
RP-D14A readiness banner?No verdict, ever. A count naming the worst signal ("1 ponto de atenção antes de decidir: …"), or "Nenhum sinal acima do limite. A decisão continua sendo sua." The focus preview of each action's outcome is optional and can follow.
RP-D15Ship the master-detail at all?Not now. After A has served a month, decide on evidence (applications decided per session, time per application). If yes, as a page per application with the list as a left column and prefetch, not a fragment, reusing A's partials.
RP-D16Inline triage in the queue?No. If a later batch need appears: confirm and defer only, cancel and adjustments on the full page.
RP-D17Where does a course of ~100 groups change the page?Only inside "Ajustar", in the participants page's bulk form and in the queue's group filter (V2). The strip, the signals, the outcome card and the bar are unchanged, because the outcome card already names the destination and most decisions never open "Ajustar". Never render a card per group.
RP-D18The threshold between cards and search8 choosable groups. Up to 8, every choosable group is a card (§04); above 8, the route's groups are pinned cards and the rest are searched. Counted after the reader's narrowing (choosable_groups()), so moderators in 1 to 3 groups keep cards and a coordinator in 20 searches their own 20 (S6). The same 8 decides a plain select or a search in the queue filter.
RP-D19Which groups are pinned, and ticked?What the approval joins and this reader may see: key_route_groups() for a keyed application, default_approval_groups() otherwise, intersected with the choosable set; ticked, unticking allowed. Note one change: for a keyless application today's chooser ticks nothing and the empty choice falls back to the method's groups, so ticking them posts the same groups explicitly and records them in decidedgroups. Same approval, a fuller record (and the S8 report narrowing then sees them); recommended, but it is a change in what is stored.
RP-D20The control for "Adicionar outro grupo"A plain <select multiple name="groups[]"> in its own wrapper, holding the choosable groups minus the pinned ones, enhanced by core/form-autocomplete enhance(selector, false, false, placeholder): client-side, no ajax module, no tags. Core uses it outside moodleform the same way (report/competency/templates/user_course_navigation.mustache, reportbuilder/amd/src/local/editor/conditions.js). Without JavaScript it is a native multi-select and the post is unchanged: groups[] from cards and select merged, read and allowlisted as today.
RP-D21The text of each option"{name} · {members} de {seats} vagas"; "{name} · sem vagas, {members} de {seats}" when full, still selectable (R5); the idnumber in parentheses after the name when set; the name alone when no seats are declared. Plain text, no data-html. "Sem vagas", not "lotada": it agrees with any group name. New strings, en and pt_br in lockstep. The chip shows the same text (core renders the option's label in the chip).
RP-D22Order of the groupsNatural order everywhere a group list is shown (cards, search, bulk form, queue filter): core_collator::asort_objects_by_property(..., 'name', core_collator::SORT_NATURAL). groups_get_all_groups() sorts by name ASC in the database's collation, which puts "Turma 10" before "Turma 2".
RP-D23GroupingsNot optgroups: core's autocomplete reads only direct <option> children and would drop every grouped option. A separate "Agrupamento" select above the search, shown only when a grouping holds a choosable group, narrowing the suggestions by core's documented data-enabled="disabled" attribute; a group in several groupings stays one option. Hidden without JavaScript. Read from the cached groups_get_course_data() mappings, so no query.
RP-D24A "Mover para outro grupo" shortcut?Not in the first slice. Untick the key's card and add the new group: two actions, both visible, and the outcome sentence names the result and adds "Não entra na Turma 37, o grupo da chave." A move control would hide the method's other groups' fate (stay or go?) behind one word. Revisit after use; if added, as a link on the key card that unticks it and focuses the search, nothing more.
RP-D25Which seats does "O que pesa na decisão" show?Only the groups the approval will join (the pinned route, RP-D2), never the course's other groups. A group added through the search carries its seats in its chip and in the outcome card's "Depois" line, which the AMD module rewrites; the server-rendered signals do not change on a tick.
RP-D26The participants page's bulk formmoodleform's own autocomplete element with 'multiple' => true, replacing the select of names (bulk_decision_form.php:165–176); the same option text (with seats, read in one groupseats::for_groups() call) and natural order. No pinned cards there: the selection spans applications with different routes, and the form already says what an empty choice gives each of them.
RP-D27The queue's group filter (V2)A single-value filter, so a single-select autocomplete above the threshold, a plain select below; names without seats. Values: a group id; none ("Sem grupo"), only for readers who see keyless applications, as V2 says; and a new mine ("Meus grupos"), only for an unnarrowed reader who belongs to at least one group: V-D2's default made a value the reader can pick back. A narrowed reader gets neither, since their scope already is their groups. Every value cleaned against the offered list on the server, as V2 specifies.
RP-D28Accessibility of the searchCore's control as it is: a role="combobox" input owning two listboxes, arrows, Enter and Escape, and the polite "Added …"/"Removed …" announcements, which read the seat text. Seat state is always text ("sem vagas", "18 de 20"), the bars stay role="progressbar" with a label. The label points at the select's id (core moves it to the input). Behat drives it only through I set the field, with and without @javascript.
