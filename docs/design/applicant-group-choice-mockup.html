Applicant group choice

Interactive mockup for docs/design/applicant-group-choice.md. One course, two enrolment methods: Apply as a student and Apply as a moderator (M2, C-D20, C-D25). Screen 1 is the course enrolment page; screen 2 the whole instance form of either method; screen 3 the application modal of the method applied to; screen 4 the review page. Everything is one state: edit a method and its card, modal and review follow. Help icons open their text; an orange ring marks a help that does not exist today.

Design switches (open decisions, not instance settings)

1 · Course enrolment page (enrol/index.php)

Two methods, two cards. The moderator card is gated by a cohort (C-D25, G-a) and, with "Hide this card" on, invisible to everybody outside it (G-b). Switch who is looking; the buttons open screen 3.

2 · Instance form, the whole page (enrol/editinstance.php)

Every section open, every field of today's form in its new place (C-D15), plus the new ones. Pick a method; with "Moderator recruitment" a purple bar marks each field that differs from the student method, which is the recipe for setting one up. The form's notices (under Groups) react to what you set: try "Who decides" back to "The deciders of the application's group".

3 · Application modal

Opened from a card on screen 1. The list shows up to eight groups with places and a note when there are more (C-D24); typing searches all, those with places first, then the full ones, disabled. On the student method with keys on (Groups, "And with a group key?"), try the key EVE-2026.

    Submit an application in the modal first. The table says who is handed it; the form below is Carla's, who sees every group.
