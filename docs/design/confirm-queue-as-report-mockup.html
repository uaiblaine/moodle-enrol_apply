What comes from the report, and what stays the queue's

  • From the report, unchanged: the bar (scope, count, Filters with its counter, Columns with its sets, View, the search that opens from its icon), the chips, the bounded grid with a sticky heading and applicant column, one column per profile field and per question, the cards with "More", the pager, and the narrow form: the scope under the controls, the count without its word, the filter panel as a bottom sheet, cards in one column.
  • Left out: Download (the queue is not a record to export; "Open the report" leads to it) and the turnaround band (the capacity tiles stand in its place).
  • The queue's own: the selection and the decision bar (approve, defer, cancel, with the message and the groups), the Deferred scope.
  • New here: the profile picture; the tags under the name (Deferred, Applied before, the key's outcome with its group, "Your own application", which has no selection box); "Earlier applications" as an optional column for a holder of the reports capability.
  • Unchanged rules: every row is one the reader may decide (group narrowing, own application, mentors); identity fields follow core's showuseridentity; an answer the reader may not see is not a column for them.
